Alongside physics, chess is an important part of my life at Sharif University of Technology. As a member of the university’s women’s chess team, I won an individual board gold at the regional championships and helped our team take silver in standard chess at the national student Olympiad.

## Board gold at the regional championships

The Region 1 University Chess Championships took place from **31 December 2023 to 2 January 2024** (10–12 Dey 1402), with six-round Swiss tournaments in standard, rapid, and blitz chess.

I won **gold on board 3 in standard chess**. Our team also finished **second in standard and rapid**, and **third in blitz**, qualifying for the 16th National Student Sports Olympiad. The team included Armita Jalalioun, Sara Orooji, Fatemeh Mehrabi, and me; Armita also won standard-chess gold on board 4.

[Read Sharif’s regional championship report (Persian)](https://sport.sharif.ir/chess-region-1-championship-girls-boys-1402).

## Team silver at the national Olympiad

In **August 2024**, I represented Sharif alongside Sara Orooji and Fatemeh Mehrabi at the **16th National Student Cultural and Sports Olympiad**. Isfahan University of Technology hosted the women’s events.

We finished **second in the women’s standard chess team competition**, earning the silver medal for Sharif. Our coach was Farzad Bolourchifard.

[Read Sharif’s Olympiad report (Persian)](https://sport.sharif.ir/16th-sports-cultural-olympiad-1403-isfahan) · [View the host university’s chess medal ceremony gallery (Persian)](https://olympiad16.iut.ac.ir/fa/node/1714).

## Recognition at Sharif

On **10 June 2025** (20 Khordad 1404), Sharif held a ceremony to recognize its Olympiad medalists, coaches, and delegation staff. Sara, Fatemeh, and I were among the chess players honored for our results at the 2024 Olympiad.

[Read Sharif’s recognition ceremony report (Persian)](https://sport.sharif.ir/w/-100).
