This undergraduate research at Sharif University of Technology connects optical modeling with biomedical applications.

## Research question

How can models of light propagation and heating help us understand laser–tissue interactions for non-invasive diagnostic applications?

## My work

- Developed computational models of optical and thermal interactions between coherent light and biological tissue.
- Performed photon transport simulations in multilayer skin models to explore clinical parameters and laser-based treatments.
