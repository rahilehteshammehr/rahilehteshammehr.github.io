The cosmic microwave background offers an observational window into the early universe.

## Research question

What can the cosmic microwave background tell us about primordial density fluctuations?

## Project work

- Examined how cosmic microwave background data provide a snapshot of primordial density fluctuations.
- Explored their connection to the structure of today’s cosmos.
