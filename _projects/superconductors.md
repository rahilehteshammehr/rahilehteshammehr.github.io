This project explored a central topic in condensed matter physics: the transition into the superconducting state.

## Research question

What approaches could raise the critical temperature of high-temperature superconductors?

## Project work

- Explored and analyzed methods for raising the critical temperature in high-temperature superconductors.
- Examined these approaches in the context of advancing practical applications.
