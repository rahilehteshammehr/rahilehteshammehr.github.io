At the Nanoparticles and Coatings Lab (NCL) at Sharif University of Technology, I work on the fabrication of perovskite solar cells, depositing the functional layers of the device using solution-based techniques such as spin coating and blade coating.

To evaluate the devices, I measure their current–voltage (I–V) characteristics and repeat these measurements over time. This lets me compare devices with one another and monitor how their performance holds up, since long-term stability remains one of the main challenges for perovskite photovoltaics.

Perovskite devices are highly sensitive to processing: small changes in deposition conditions affect film morphology and coverage, and ultimately device performance. Working hands-on with both fabrication and characterization has given me practical experience in how processing choices translate into device behavior.
