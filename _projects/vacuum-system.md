The project links vacuum technology to scanning electron microscopy (SEM) and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS).

## Research question

How can a vacuum system be designed and optimized for nanoscale surface analysis?

## Project work

- Worked on the design, optimization, and testing of a high-vacuum system for SEM and XPS instruments.
- Considered ultra-high-vacuum conditions as a design goal for improving surface-analysis accuracy and resolution.
