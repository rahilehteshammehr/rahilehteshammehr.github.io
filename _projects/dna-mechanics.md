## Research question

How do confinement and tensile forces alter the apparent persistence length of DNA?

## Project work

- Quantified the effect of mechanical confinement and tensile forces on apparent DNA persistence length.
- Analyzed deviations from worm-like chain model predictions under constrained conditions.
